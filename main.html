Paris


Paris is the capital city of France and is famous for its beautiful architecture, romantic atmosphere, world-class museums, and delicious food. It is one of the most visited tourist destinations in the world.

Top 5 attractions

  1. Eiffel Tower – The most iconic landmark in Paris.
  2. Louvre Museum – Home to the famous Mona Lisa painting.
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – A historic Gothic cathedral.
  4. Champs-Élysées – A popular shopping and entertainment street.
  5. Seine River Cruise – A relaxing way to explore the city.

Must-Try foods

  • Croissant – A buttery and flaky French pastry.
  • Macarons – Colorful sweet sandwich cookies.
  • French Onion Soup – A classic French soup dish.
  • Crêpes – Thin pancakes served with sweet or savory fillings.
  • Baguette – Traditional French bread loved worldwide.
